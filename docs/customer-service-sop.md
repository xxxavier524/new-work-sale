# T5 客服 SOP + 英文模板库

> 对应 PROJECT_PLAN §9-T5。目标：top 10 咨询场景 90% 可模板化回复。
> **政策锚点（§4.1-C4 差异化）**：小件问题**直接补发、不要求退回**（补发成本 < 争议成本 < 差评成本）。
> 回复 SLA：24h 内（澳洲时区醒着的时间优先处理）。签名统一用 `[Store Name] Support — Sydney time zone friendly`。

## 政策速查

| 场景 | 政策 | 上限 |
|---|---|---|
| 质量问题（扣具/缝线/锈） | 直接补发新品，不要求退回，不扯皮 | 同订单补发 1 次；二次问题全额退款 |
| 尺寸/车型不匹配 | 先给适配建议；确不匹配 → 全额退款不退货（小件）| 单件 ≤A$50 |
| 未妥投/物流超时 | >25 天未妥投直接补发或退款，客户二选一 | — |
| 主观不喜欢 | 30 天退款；A$20 以下直接退不退货 | — |
| 改地址/取消 | 未出库即改/取消；已出库如实说明 | — |

## 英文模板（10 场景）

### 1. Where is my order? (物流查询)
> Hi {name}, thanks for reaching out! Your order shipped on {date} and is currently in transit — typical delivery to {state} is 7–15 business days. Track it here: {tracking_url}. If it hasn't arrived by {date+15}, message me and I'll sort it out immediately — replacement or refund, your choice. Cheers!

### 2. Delivery overdue (超时未达)
> Hi {name}, sorry about the wait — that's longer than it should be. I'm not going to make you chase the courier: if it doesn't arrive by {date}, I'll send a free replacement or full refund, whichever you prefer. Just reply with your pick. Cheers!

### 3. Broken clip / hardware issue (质量问题 → 直接补发)
> Hi {name}, that's not the experience we promise — sorry! No need to return anything or send it back. I've already arranged a replacement to ship out within 24 hours (tracking to follow). Keep the original as a spare for parts. If anything else comes up, I'm one message away. Cheers!

### 4. Will it fit my car? (车型适配 FAQ)
> Hi {name}, great question! This organiser is cut specifically for {Hilux N80 (2015+) / Ranger PX/T6 rear seats}. If you're running a different model, the MOLLE panel dimensions are {W}×{H} cm — happy to check against your seat if you tell me the model and year. If it doesn't fit after purchase, refund's on me, no return needed. Cheers!

### 5. Squid jig sink rate / how to use (产品使用)
> Hi {name}, each batch is tank-tested before packing — the measured sink rate is printed on the pack ({size 2.5: ~5.0 s/m, 3.0: ~3.5 s/m, 3.5: ~3.0 s/m}). For {location}-style shallow grounds start with the 2.5, count it down, and slow-lift + pause. The stainless prongs won't rust like the cheap ones — rinse in fresh water and store them in the drainage wallet damp-free. Tight lines!

### 6. Return request — small item (退货请求 → 退款不退回)
> Hi {name}, sorry it wasn't right for you. Here's the easy version: I've issued a full refund — no need to post anything back (shipping it back costs you more than it's worth). If you know a mate who'd get use out of it, pass it on. Thanks for giving us a go!

### 7. Negative review / dispute de-escalation (差评安抚)
> Hi {name}, I read your review and you're right to be annoyed — that's on us. I've {sent a replacement / issued a full refund} already, no strings attached. If the fix lands well and you feel like updating the review, that would mean a lot to a small store; if not, no hard feelings — your feedback already went to the factory as a change order. Cheers!

### 8. Customs / GST question (税费咨询)
> Hi {name}, nothing owed on delivery — GST was already collected at checkout by eBay, and items under A$1,000 clear customs without extra charges. The price you paid is the total. Cheers!

### 9. Cancel / change address (取消或改地址)
> Hi {name}, got it! Your order hasn't left the warehouse yet, so I've {cancelled it and refunded in full / updated the address to: ...}. If it had already shipped I would've told you straight and we'd have worked out the fastest fix. Cheers!

### 10. Wholesale / bulk enquiry (批发咨询)
> Hi {name}, thanks for the interest! For 10+ units I can do {X}% off, shipped in one consignment. Tell me the quantity and postcode and I'll quote you landed. Cheers!

## 升级线（模板搞不定时）

1. 金额 >A$100 或客户二次不满 → 脱离模板人工处理，当天回复
2. 提及 ACCC / chargeback / 律师 → 立即全额退款止损，记录进周报
3. 同一 SKU 同类问题 7 天内 ≥3 起 → 触发批次质量报警，写工厂改进单（§6 D61 动作）
